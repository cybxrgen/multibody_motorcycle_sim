## 5. Findings worth keeping
- Several first-draft test expectations were physically wrong and were corrected after measurement, not by loosening: a sliding mass in a rotating pair is not `s = v t` (centrifugal terms); a rotation of two COM-offset bodies about a distant axis is not force-free for an isolated pair; the damping of the first step after hand-applied impulses sees constraint-inconsistent velocities (~3x) because forces are evaluated before the solver (settled systems do not); the lower-part momentum after an impulse pair is therefore not the injected impulse.
- The longer front chain converges slower (front-axle pivot residual 4.9e-3 m/s at 100 iterations, 6.5e-4 at 200; registration order changes it about 3x), so the M2.1 file now uses 200 iterations.
- The suite is about 3x slower (138 s vs 41 s), mostly six-body runs; the regression battery runs at 100 iterations and the run lengths were trimmed to what each assertion needs.
