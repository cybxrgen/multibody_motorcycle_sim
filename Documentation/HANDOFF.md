## 3. What changed
- `Simulation/Motorcycle/tire_patch_normal_support.gd`: `axle_principal_axis_error(local_inertia)` now evaluates `rho = |I u - u (u^T I u)| / |I u|` with `u = (1, 0, 0)` in the body frame, i.e. `sqrt(J_xy^2 + J_xz^2) / sqrt(I_xx^2 + J_xy^2 + J_xz^2)` with the symmetrised products of inertia, and rejects when `rho > AXLE_PRINCIPAL_RESIDUAL_TOL = 1e-4` (the constant `AXLE_PRINCIPAL_REL_TOL` is gone). The components are divided by their largest magnitude first (scale-independent, no overflow); a non-finite entry, `I u = 0` or `I_xx <= 0` yields a `nan` residual and is reported, never accepted. The message keeps its requirement and remedy text and now reports `I_xx`, `I_xy`, `I_xz`, the residual and the tolerance. Nothing is projected, diagonalised or repaired.
- `Tests/test_tire_patch_normal_support.gd`: tolerance literal renamed and set to 1e-4; `test_o_validation_result_and_message_are_deterministic` reads the new message (the residual is parsed from the text and compared numerically); the O5 boundary tests are unchanged in structure (residual at half and twice the tolerance). New: `test_o_high_anisotropy_wheel_with_a_small_axial_moment_is_rejected_by_the_residual` and `test_o_the_residual_is_relative_to_the_axial_row_and_unusable_tensors_are_reported`. The fixture of the first is `I = [[1, 0.004, -0.003], [0.004, 1000, 0], [-0.003, 0, 1000.5]]` kg m^2: valid inertia, residual 4.99994e-3 (50 times the tolerance), accepted by the replaced criterion (reproduced in the test file; its tolerance was 0.010005 against a coupling of 0.005).
- Documentation: `PHYSICS_CONVENTIONS.md` (residual definition, why it bounds the effective-mass error, tolerance and its float32 basis, unusable tensors, rejected approach 4), `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `TESTING.md`, `PROJECT_STATE.md` (limitation bullet, verification record), this file. Not changed: `PHASE_PLAN.md`, the solver, rows, `SimMassProperties`, `MotorcycleConfig`, scenes.
