## 3. What changed
- New module `Simulation/Motorcycle/` (no change to `Simulation/Core` or `Simulation/Solver`): `motorcycle_body_spec.gd`, `motorcycle_joint_spec.gd` (plain data), `motorcycle_config.gd` (`MotorcycleConfig`: five body specs, four joint specs, wheel dimensions, `validate()`, `wheel_inertia()`), `motorcycle_builder.gd` (`MotorcycleBuilder.build(config, world = null)`, `hinge_reference()`, `construction_error()`), `motorcycle_assembly.gd` (named bodies and joints, queries), `motorcycle_prototype.gd` (illustrative fixture values, explicitly not calibrated). Hinges are created with `HingeJointConstraint.from_world`, so local anchors/axes/reference vectors are derived from world geometry; the fork frame (rake) and swingarm frame (tilt) are rotated so the bodies do not share local orientations. Failure policy: null + `push_error`, complete rollback of exactly what the call registered.
- `Tests/test_motorcycle_assembly.gd`: 28 tests (construction/validity incl. 20 invalid-config cases and registration-failure injection, kinematics, DOF probes, coupled dynamics and conservation, free fall, determinism, regression sensitivity). Tolerances and their measured values/mechanisms are inline and in `TESTING.md`.
- Presentation (additive): `Simulation/Presentation/body_visual_proxy.gd` and `simulation_renderer.gd` accept an optional mesh offset and `add_mesh`; new `Scenes/motorcycle_test.tscn/.gd` (zero gravity; zero-momentum initial relative rotations applied once at scenario build; reads published state only).
- Documentation: `PHASE_PLAN.md` (replaced: M1 complete, M2 current, M2.1 implemented, remaining M2 work listed, not complete), `PROJECT_STATE.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` (D18–D20), `TESTING.md`, this file. `PHYSICS_CONVENTIONS.md` and `AGENTS.md` untouched (no physics convention was established or changed; the sign conventions belong to the fixture).

## 5. Unresolved / limitations
- The assembly is deliberately incomplete: free hinges without limits, no ground, suspension, tires or drivetrain; it neither stands nor rides (`PROJECT_STATE.md`, "Motorcycle assembly (M2.1)").
- The default `solver_iterations` of 20 leaves visible velocity-row residuals for this chain under violent excitation (tests and measurements use 100); whether the demonstration or later milestones should raise it is undecided.
- `MotorcycleBuilder.construction_error()` could not be tripped by any build input; it is tested by displacing a built assembly.
