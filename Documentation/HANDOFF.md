## 5. Findings worth keeping
- Several first-draft expectations were wrong and were fixed after measurement, not by loosening: the velocity-first scheme is the exact solution shifted by h/2 (the continuous-solution bound needed that term); a hand-applied accumulator before `step()` doubles the force; an anisotropic body spun to ~6 rad/s in one step differs ~4 % from `h I^-1 tau` because of the midpoint gyroscopic term (the test uses a spherical swingarm); total kinetic energy of an isolated damped pair keeps the centre-of-mass part, so decay is judged on the internal energy; with no ground, absolute velocities in gravity grow without bound, so sustained-run bounds use relative motion.
- The general-geometry energy error is first order in h and large at 1/120 s for the light swingarm (0.107 of E0 over 4 s for the test pair): the explicit evaluation is time-step limited (`PHYSICS_CONVENTIONS.md`).
- Two sign mistakes in the first integration tests (rest length 0.9 l is a STRETCHED shock; the first kick lengthens it) were test errors, not component defects.
